# Phase 1 (Discover), step 1.4: Demand signals and a Mom Test interview script

Status: **LIVE**. Model: `ollama/gpt-oss:20b`. Brief: Notion templates that help freelance video editors track client revisions, feedback rounds and deadlines in one place

## Conclusion

The interview script is a viable, low‑cost way to validate demand for a Notion template pack among freelance video editors.

## Supporting points

- The target users already juggle multiple clients and need better revision tracking (C1.4-1). (SOURCED: C1.4-1)
- Freelancers often miss billable revisions, so a tool that surfaces out‑of‑scope feedback could capture revenue (C1.4-2). (ASSUMPTION: C1.4-2)
- Users require a comprehensive system that tracks revisions, approvals, tasks, deadlines, and capacity (C1.4-4). (SOURCED: C1.4-4)
- The founder can execute the interview within the 10‑hour weekly limit using existing skills, but should keep it qualitative to stay within budget. (ASSUMPTION)
- If quantitative data is needed, additional time or budget will be required. (ASSUMPTION)

## Work

- **demand_signals:**
  - signal: Freelance video editors balancing several edits and feedback deadlines; claim_id: C1.4-1
  - signal: Scope‑verdicted feedback rounds: out‑of‑scope revisions automatically surface as billable – the money most freelancers forget to invoice; claim_id: C1.4-2
  - signal: Revision rounds, approvals, tasks, deadlines, and team‑capacity context are needed; claim_id: C1.4-4
- **interview_script:**
  - 1. Can you walk me through a recent project where you had to manage multiple clients at once?
  - 2. How do you currently keep track of revisions, approvals, and deadlines across those projects?
  - 3. What tools or methods do you use to remember which revisions are billable and which are not?
  - 4. Have you ever missed a billable revision or deadline? If so, what happened?
  - 5. How do you feel about using a single workspace (like Notion) to consolidate all that information?
  - 6. What would make that workspace easier or more useful for you?
  - 7. Have you tried any templates or systems before? What worked or didn’t work?
  - 8. If a ready‑made template could save you time on tracking revisions, would you consider using it? Why or why not?
  - 9. What price point would you find acceptable for a template that solves these problems?
  - 10. Would you be willing to join a waitlist to try such a template? Why or why not?
- **who_to_interview:** Freelance video editors who currently juggle multiple clients and use Notion or similar tools for project management
