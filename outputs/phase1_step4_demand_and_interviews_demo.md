- **interview_script:**
  - 1. Can you walk me through a recent project where you had multiple clients and revisions?
  - 2. How did you keep track of what revisions were requested for each client?
  - 3. What tools or methods did you use to remember which version of a cut a particular feedback referred to?
  - 4. Have you ever had to bill a client for an out‑of‑scope revision? How did you handle it?
  - 5. What does your weekly workflow look like when you’re juggling several projects?
  - 6. Do you use Notion or any other free tools to manage your projects? If so, what do you like or dislike about them?
  - 7. When you need to follow up on overdue invoices, what steps do you take?
  - 8. If you had a ready‑made template that could help you track revisions, deadlines, and billing, how would that change your day‑to‑day work?
  - 9. What would make you consider signing up for a waitlist for such a template?
  - 10. Is there anything that would make you hesitant to use a Notion‑based solution?
