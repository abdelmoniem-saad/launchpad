- **interview_script:**
  - 1. Can you walk me through a recent project where you had to manage multiple clients at once?
  - 2. How do you currently keep track of each client’s revisions and feedback?
  - 3. What tools or methods do you use to remember deadlines and version history?
  - 4. Have you ever missed a deadline or paid extra because a revision was overlooked? What happened?
  - 5. When a client asks for a change that isn’t in the original scope, how do you handle that?
  - 6. What would make it easier for you to see at a glance which revisions are still pending and which are billable?
  - 7. If you had a single place that organized all client communications, revisions, and deadlines, how would that change your workflow?
  - 8. Have you tried any Notion templates or similar tools for this purpose? What did you like or dislike?
  - 9. How much time do you spend each week on administrative tasks related to client revisions?
  - 10. Would you be willing to try a new tool that could reduce that time? Why or why not?
  - 11. If you found a tool that saved you 2 hours a week, would you consider paying for it?
  - 12. What price point would feel fair for a tool that helps you manage revisions and deadlines?
  - 13. How do you usually discover new tools or resources for your workflow?
  - 14. Would you join a waitlist to try a new Notion template pack for revision tracking? Why or why not?
  - 15. Is there anything else you’d like to share about managing revisions with multiple clients?
