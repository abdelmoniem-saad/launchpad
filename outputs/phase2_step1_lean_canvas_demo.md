## Work

- **problem:** Freelance video editors juggling multiple clients struggle to track revisions, feedback rounds and deadlines in a single place.
- **customer_segments:** Freelance video editors who manage several clients simultaneously, primarily English‑speaking, global.
- **unique_value_proposition:** A ready‑made Notion template pack that consolidates revision tracking, feedback loops and deadline management, enabling editors to stay organized without learning new software.
- **solution:** Create a set of Notion templates (project tracker, revision log, deadline calendar) tailored to video‑editing workflows, with pre‑filled fields and simple instructions.
- **channels:** Direct outreach via freelance video‑editing forums, LinkedIn groups, Reddit communities, and a landing page with a waitlist form.
- **revenue_streams:** Future paid template pack sales (one‑time purchase).
- **cost_structure:** $300 budget for minimal marketing (ads, landing page hosting) and 10 hrs/week of founder time for template design and outreach.
- **key_metrics:** Waitlist sign‑ups, email open rates, click‑through to landing page, feedback from initial interviews.
- **unfair_advantage:** Founder’s personal experience in video editing and Notion, combined with honest marketing and a simple, no‑dark‑pattern approach.
