## Work

- **problem:** Freelance video editors juggling multiple clients struggle to track revisions, feedback rounds, and deadlines efficiently.
- **customer_segments:**
  - Freelance video editors who manage several clients simultaneously, primarily English‑speaking, globally
- **unique_value_proposition:** A ready‑made Notion template pack that consolidates revision tracking, feedback loops, and deadline management into a single, simple workspace, saving editors time and reducing miscommunication.
- **solution:**
  - Notion template pack with pre‑built databases for clients, projects, revisions, and deadlines
  - Embedded checklists and status columns
  - Automated reminders via Notion’s native notifications
  - Exportable summary sheets for client hand‑off
- **channels:**
  - Organic search (SEO for "video editor revision tracking"), Notion template marketplace listings, niche forums (Reddit r/VideoEditing, r/VideoEditors), LinkedIn groups for freelancers, targeted Twitter threads
  - Waitlist landing page with clear value proposition
- **revenue_streams:**
  - One‑time purchase of the template pack (USD 29)
  - Optional add‑on: 1‑hour onboarding video tutorial (USD 9)
- **cost_structure:**
  - $200 for freelance copywriter to craft landing page copy
  - $50 for minimal paid ads to drive initial traffic
  - $50 for Notion template marketplace listing fee
  - $0 for hosting (free landing page builder)
  - $0 for product development (founder’s existing skills)
- **key_metrics:**
  - Number of waitlist sign‑ups
  - Conversion rate from waitlist to paid purchase
  - Average time saved per editor (self‑reported)
  - Customer satisfaction score (post‑purchase survey)
- **unfair_advantage:** Founder’s deep experience in video editing and Notion, combined with honest marketing and a focus on simple tools, creates a trustworthy brand that resonates with the niche audience.
