## Work

- **problem:** Freelance video editors juggle multiple clients, revisions, and deadlines, often lacking a unified system to track feedback and progress.
- **customer_segments:** Freelance video editors who manage several clients simultaneously, primarily English‑speaking, globally.
- **unique_value_proposition:** A ready‑made Notion template pack that consolidates revision tracking, feedback rounds, and deadline management into one simple, editable workspace, saving editors time and reducing miscommunication.
- **solution:**
  - One‑page Notion dashboard for each project
  - Pre‑built tables for revision rounds, comments, and due dates
  - Automated reminders via Notion reminders
  - Exportable status reports for clients
- **channels:**
  - Organic search (SEO for "video editor revision tracker")
  - Freelance marketplaces (posting on Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer)
  - LinkedIn posts targeting video editing groups
  - Email outreach to known freelance communities
- **revenue_streams:** One‑time purchase of the template pack (USD 29)
- **cost_structure:** Founder time (10 hrs/week for 4 weeks = 40 hrs), minimal hosting costs (Notion free tier), small marketing spend (ads or outreach tools)
- **key_metrics:**
  - Number of waitlist sign‑ups
  - Conversion rate from waitlist to paid customers
  - Time saved per project (self‑reported)
  - Customer satisfaction score
- **unfair_advantage:** Founder’s deep expertise in video editing and Notion, combined with honest, simple marketing, ensures a trustworthy, high‑quality product that resonates with the niche.
