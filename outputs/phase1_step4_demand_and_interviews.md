- **interview_script:**
  - 1. Can you walk me through a recent project where you had to manage multiple revisions from a client?
  - 2. How do you currently keep track of feedback rounds and deadlines for that project?
  - 3. What tools or methods do you use to store and organize client feedback and revision history?
  - 4. Have you ever missed a deadline or had a misunderstanding because of how feedback was tracked? What happened?
  - 5. When a client sends new feedback, how do you decide what to act on first?
  - 6. Do you use any shared documents or platforms (e.g., Notion, Google Docs, Trello) to manage this process? If so, what do you like or dislike about them?
  - 7. Have you ever paid for a service or tool specifically to help with revision tracking or project management? If yes, what was it and how did it help?
  - 8. If you had a single place that automatically logged each feedback round, deadline, and revision status, how would that change your workflow?
  - 9. What would make you consider switching to a new tool or template for this purpose?
  - 10. Would you be willing to try a prototype template and share your thoughts?
