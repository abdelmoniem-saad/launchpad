# firm.engagement_manager

The engagement manager is the founder's single point of contact. It runs without a language model:
- checks the brief is complete (idea, target customer, budget, weekly hours, skills) and interviews
  the founder for anything missing;
- runs the excluded-product check and stops, with the reason, for physical goods, financial or crypto
  products, health advice or legal advice;
- opens and closes every founder checkpoint (approve, edit, redo with feedback, stop);
- makes sure every report leads with a conclusion, and flags a report that has none instead of padding it.
